# Hindi (hi) glossary for Pardes Guard

Register: everyday spoken Hindustani in Devanagari, "आप", full stop "।". Buttons use the polite
imperative (करें, देखें, जाँचें). Where a verb would show gender, use a neutral form: imperative,
"आपको ... है", passive ("किया जा सकता है"), or "ने" constructions. Digits 0-9 only.

## Open questions for a native reviewer

- `who.employer, field.employer_name`: कंपनी does not fit someone who works for a household; मालिक is the other choice.
- `cases.title, cnew.create`: मामला follows the brief, but people usually say "केस".
- `vault.fingerprint`: Is डिजिटल कोड clear, or would डिजिटल फ़िंगरप्रिंट be better?
- `home.sos.title`: फँसे हैं is the polite masculine plural; no natural neutral form was found.
- `stamp.green`: ठीक लगता है is three words; the stamp wants one or two.
- `nav.more, more.title`: Changed to ज़्यादा (Google's Hindi for "More"); और could be read as "and".
- `sos.whatsapp.text, case.emergency.message`: Is प्लीज़ the right tone for a message to family?
- `step.complaint_regulator, term.protector.d`: Office names are described or transliterated; there is no Hindi name to use.
- `purpose.skilled_migration`: स्किल्ड माइग्रेशन may be unclear to readers with little schooling.
- `spelling`: The nukta is left off क़ (कानून, रकम, कतर) but kept on ख़, ग़, ज़ and फ़.
