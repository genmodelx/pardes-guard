# Bengali (bn, Bangladesh) glossary for Pardes Guard

Register: standard colloquial Bengali (চলিত ভাষা) as used in Bangladesh. Reader is "আপনি";
polite imperatives (-উন: করুন, দেখুন, দিন, নিন, রাখুন); negative "করবেন না", "দেবেন না".
Full stop "।". Digits always 0-9. Spelling as in Bangladesh: সরকারি, জরুরি, তৈরি, কোম্পানি,
ইংরেজি, কোনো, এখনো, দেওয়া, নেওয়া, সাথে. Button and UI names inside a sentence go in ‘ ’.
Quotes that the English has are also written as ‘ ’.

## Stamps

STOP = থামুন · CHECK = যাচাই করুন · LOOKS OK = ঠিক লাগছে · DONE = সম্পন্ন

## Placeholders

Bengali case endings change with the last sound of a word, so a placeholder never takes a
case ending when that can be avoided: "{country}: ...", "এই অফিসে জিজ্ঞেস করুন: {office}",
"{name} নিয়ে", "{paper} অনুযায়ী", "‘{title}’ অভিযোগের". Numbers and amounts take "-এর".
Product names stay in Latin letters with a hyphenated ending: Google-এর, WhatsApp-এ, Gemini-তে.

## Open questions for a native reviewer

- `cases.title and every case string`: অভিযোগ avoids কেস/মামলা (which suggest a court case), but may suggest that opening a case files a complaint.
- `vault.fingerprint`: ডিজিটাল ছাপ: confirm nobody reads it as a finger. The fallback is ফাইল কোড.
- `stamp.green, stamp.done`: ঠিক লাগছে keeps the stamp to two words; সম্পন্ন on the stamp but শেষ for step states.
- `term.promoter, app.checkAgent.d, rec.lead, rec.official`: The Bangladeshi term লাইসেন্সধারী রিক্রুটিং এজেন্সি is used instead of প্রমোটার.
- `term.iqama`: আকামা is what Bangladeshi workers say; ইকামা is the formal spelling.
- `edu.secondary, edu.higher_secondary`: SSC and HSC (এসএসসি, এইচএসসি) instead of Matric and Intermediate.
- `step.complaint_regulator`: অভিবাসন কর্তৃপক্ষ may read as "immigration authority".
- `who.employer`: নিয়োগকর্তা is formal; workers often say মালিক or কোম্পানি.
- `sos.whatsapp.text`: The message to family uses আপনি; many families use তুমি.
- `check.statementPh`: The example keeps 450,000 রুপি as in English; Bangladeshi readers think in টাকা.
