# Nepali (ne) glossary for Pardes Guard

Standard Nepali as used in Nepal, Devanagari, digits 0-9, full stop "।".
Reader is addressed as "तपाईं" with polite "-नुहोस्" forms (gender-neutral).
Spelling style: popular forms with anusvara (तपाईं, छौं, संकेत, अंक); first-person
singular past with chandrabindu (पाएँ, जाँचेँ, पठाइसकेँ). Postpositions after a
placeholder are written with a space: "{country} मा", "{date} मा".

## Open questions for a native reviewer

- `vault.fingerprint and every fingerprint string`: Will people read डिजिटल छाप as a file code, not a finger?
- `cases.title and every case string`: मामला can sound legal; उजुरी is the other choice.
- `edu.secondary, edu.higher_secondary`: Nepal's levels (एसईई, प्लस टु) instead of Matric and Intermediate.
- `app.checkAgent.d, rec.lead, rec.official, term.promoter.d`: "Licensed promoters" became लाइसेन्स भएका एजेन्ट; "recruiting company" became म्यानपावर कम्पनी.
- `settings.share.help, privacy.shared.d`: Is उल्टोपाल्टो पारिएका कोड a clear way to say "scrambled codes"?
- `badge.draft`: ड्राफ्ट, or the more formal मस्यौदा?
- `stamp.red`: रोक्नुहोस् may be long for the round stamp.
- `signin.notSetUp and "this copy of the app"`: कपी also means "notebook".
- `flag.VISA_TYPE_MISMATCH, next.workVisa`: The spoken भिजिट भिसा is used rather than भ्रमण भिसा.
- `step.complaint_regulator`: "Emigration regulator" became वैदेशिक रोजगार हेर्ने सरकारी कार्यालय.
