# Filipino (fil) glossary for Pardes Guard

Register: everyday Filipino/Taglish as OFWs and their families speak it. Friendly singular
"ka / mo / iyo" everywhere, no "po". "Kami / namin" (exclusive) for the app and our team
("ang team namin"). Spelling: puwede, kompanya, lisensya, ebidensya, sahod.

## Fixed button and label names quoted inside sentences

Speak = Magsalita; Try again = Subukan ulit; Check now = Suriin ngayon;
Delete everything = Burahin lahat; More = Iba pa.

## Stamps (round stamp, capitals)

STOP = HINTO; CHECK = SURIIN; LOOKS OK = MUKHANG OK; DONE = TAPOS NA.

## Open questions for a native reviewer

- `tagline`: "fixer" is used for "middleman", the Philippine word for a paid go-between.
- `nav.journey`: Paglalakbay may read as just "travel"; the options are "Journey" or "Biyahe".
- `nav.check, stamp.amber`: "Suriin", or the Taglish "I-check"?
- `who.recruiter and every agent string`: "agency" also covers individual recruiters, whom people may call "ahente".
- `sos.case, event.emergency`: "emergency na reklamo": does "reklamo" fit an emergency case?
- `vault.title`: "taguan" can suggest a hiding place.
- `vault.fingerprint, field.layout_hash`: Are "code ng file" and "code ng layout" clear enough?
- `edu.secondary, edu.higher_secondary`: Set to "High school / Grade 10" and "Senior high school / Grade 12"; check the mapping.
- `home.sos.title`: "naipit" for "stuck abroad"; "na-stranded" is the alternative.
- `severity.low`: "Hindi gaanong seryoso" is long for a chip.
